About

I'm a finance researcher with nearly a decade of experience across academia and industry, working as a researcher, analyst and consultant. My research looks at how ownership, governance and legal frameworks shape corporate and investor decisions, and I increasingly connect corporate finance with asset pricing and portfolio choice.

My path runs from management at V.N. Karazin Kharkiv National University (BSc, MSc), through an MSc in Finance at the Higher School of Economics, to an MRes and PhD in Finance at Queen Mary University of London. I became a full ACCA member in 2020 and spent part of my career in Deloitte's Financial Forensic Investigation team. I'm constantly expanding my analytical toolkit through advanced data analysis, sophisticated estimation techniques and programming for data processing and modelling.

Away from the desk, you'll find me on the tennis court, which gives me the perfect mix of fitness, strength and agility. Since 2019 I've also sung as a baritone with the Royal Choral Society, performing in some world-class venues and concert halls.

Research interests

My research spans finance, economics and corporate law. I started in management (corporate and strategic management) and moved to corporate finance, governance, ownership, and labour and political economics. I focus on applied finance and applied microeconomics; my current work sits between corporate finance and asset pricing.

Topics: Corporate Finance, Asset Pricing, ESG, Sustainable (Climate) and Biodiversity Finance, Institutional Investors, Portfolio Management, Emerging Markets.

Job market paper: Policy Uncertainty and Institutional ESG Investments: U.S. Mutual Funds Portfolio Rebalancing
